This counts room pickups, not her starting 15 loaded rounds, and not
carry-over from the previous act. She starts with no spare handgun rounds. Enemy counts are the
spawn totals in day/foes.ts. No new enemies are added.

The act 3 starting count includes the 15 rounds loaded in Kevin's old
Beretta pickup. Its replacement is a green herb, beside the existing clip.
She keeps her own pistol all day and leaves the rest of her gear in the
crawl space, so there is no replacement gun and no midgame inventory reset
to compensate for.

Act 2 has eleven zombies, four dogs and three spinners. Those eighteen
larger enemies get a budget of 4.17 new rounds each, up from 3.33. The
other eighteen enemies are twelve crows and six adders. They are intended
to be avoided when possible. This is a pickup budget, not a promise that
every larger enemy must be killed or takes the same number of hits.

The only extra clip is at the optional mast detour, together with its
planned first aid spray. It rewards taking the lift and exploring the
summit. A green herb in Hal's cottage gives one more chance to heal before
entering the mine. There are no extra caches to recover from a gallery loss.

The cellar's clip becomes five loose handgun rounds for its one zombie.
The optional wine-store spray becomes one green herb and its plan step
says so. The final approach stays sparse before she leaves her pack at the
crawl turn. She keeps the Beretta and its loaded rounds, and key items.
Spare rounds, other weapons and all carried healing go there once.

None of this balance has been played. Route choice, aim, damage taken,
the number of enemies she can safely avoid, and rounds left at the final
loss still need an in-game pass.

## Act 1 after the ridge review

Rebecca starts with the Beretta loaded with 15 rounds and the knife. There
are no handgun pickups in act 1. The total falls from 30 to 15 rounds;
no other room supplies change.

Act 1 has eight spawns: two crows on the trail, one Cerberus in the yard,
three adders at the bridge, and two crows at the platform. That is 1.875
rounds per spawn. If she avoids all four crows and three adders, she has
15 rounds for the yard dog. The woods and den dogs belong to act 2.
The cabin has one first aid spray; the booth has one green herb. Neither
adds ammunition.

This is a tight avoidance budget. The source tables do not give enemy HP
or hit counts, so they cannot establish that 15 rounds are enough for every
encounter. The ridge recording checks the starting kit and the exit;
it does not test act 1 combat. A route test with normal damage is still
needed to judge ammunition sufficiency. No supplies have been rebalanced.
